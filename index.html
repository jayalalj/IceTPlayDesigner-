How it works

  1. Coach designsPick a starting position, drag players to where they end up at each step, and write one line per step.
  2. Coach sharesCopy the share link and paste it into an email or the team chat. The play is inside the link.
  3. Team playsPlayers tap the link on any phone or laptop, pick their position, Watch, then Play it and get graded.

How it works
  1. Pick a faceoff dot or a situation, or press 📍 Place puck and tap the ice. Drag anyone who isn't where you want them.
  2. Press + Add step, then drag players to where they are at the end of that step. Faded players show where they were.
  3. Write one line for each step. That's the caption the kids read.
  4. Test it opens the play the way the team sees it: Watch, Play it and grading.
  5. Copy share link and paste it into an email or the team chat. Players open it and play.
